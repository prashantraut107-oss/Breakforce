FAQs

Before you request a quote.

How is concrete-breaking work priced?

Pricing depends on the concrete type and thickness, reinforcement, quantity, access, working height, equipment, working hours and debris handling. A quotation should be confirmed after reviewing the job.

Do you work in Pune?

Enquiries from Pune and nearby areas are welcome. Availability and travel costs are confirmed according to the project scope and schedule.

What information should I share?

Share the site location, approximate dimensions, concrete thickness if known, photos or video, access details and whether debris removal is required.

Can any RCC member be broken?

No. Beams, columns, structural slabs and other load-bearing elements require review and written approval from the responsible structural engineer before work.
